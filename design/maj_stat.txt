## Simulation Context & Parameters
- Starting hands (14 tiles) simulated: 500,000,000
- Execution time: 5363.3 seconds (93,226 hands/sec)
- Rules: Riichi = yes, Tsumo = yes, Round wind = random (East/South), Player wind = random.
- Shanten calculation: calculated after the best discard. 
- Category assignment: based on the best possible hand after (shanten + 1) draws.

## Glossary & Column Definitions
- **Shanten**: Number of tiles away from Tenpai (2-shanten, 1-shanten, Tenpai).
- **Hand Maximum**: The highest value hand category achievable.
- **Count**: Number of hands in this category.
- **% Inside**: Percentage within this specific shanten category.
- **% of All**: Percentage of all 500,000,000 simulated hands.
- **Cumulative >=**: Cumulative percentage (from top to bottom).
